# Wandering Collector Reborn

Lost your gear in lava, or did it despawn before you got back to your grave? Wandering Traders remember it. Next time one shows up, it offers to sell you some of your lost items back, once each, for emeralds.

Port of [Wandering Collector](https://github.com/Siphalor/wandering-collector) by Siphalor to Minecraft 1.21.1, for NeoForge and Fabric. Files carried over from the original keep their Apache-2.0 headers, with a note that they were modified for this port.

## Building

`./gradlew buildAll` builds both loaders and runs the game tests.
